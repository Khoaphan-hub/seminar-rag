[Bùi Trọng Thành · 65 seconds] Finally, here is how these lessons could scale to an organization. This is a proposal from Appendix B of our report, not something we built or evaluated. Documents are versioned, extracted and indexed, and a user question may be rewritten into subqueries. Lexical and dense retrieval both supply candidates, which are merged and reranked. The key decision is whether every requested fact has enough evidence. If it does, the system generates a cited answer. If not, it retrieves more and checks again, or states the gap. The output is then verified claim by claim, and feedback supports evaluation. Each addition answers something we observed: wording sensitivity points to query rewriting and hybrid retrieval, related but unhelpful passages point to reranking, and missing or unsupported facts point to evidence checks and claim verification.
