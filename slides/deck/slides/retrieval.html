[Bùi Trọng Thành · 65 seconds] Here are the 192-token results. At every k, TF-IDF recalls more relevant chunks than MiniLM. At k equal to three, which we also used for generation, it reaches 0.80 against 0.60. The table shows the same pattern for hit rate, evidence recall and all-evidence. Why might this happen? Our questions contain exact terms from the paper, such as class names, license names, NNZ and AUC, and that plausibly favors word matching. But this is an interpretation, because we did not separate the effects of the model, the question wording and preprocessing. MiniLM also wins on individual questions, including Q06 and Q13. So we do not claim that lexical retrieval is generally better. Lewis and colleagues found dense retrieval better on almost every task, with FEVER as a small exception.
