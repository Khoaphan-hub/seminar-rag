[Bùi Trọng Thành · 100 seconds] We sent the same twenty questions to Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite under three conditions, for sixty responses in total. All conditions received the same paper title, authors and year, so the closed-book baseline was not penalized for not knowing which paper we meant. Both RAG conditions used 192-token chunks and k equal to three. Closed-book was fully correct on five questions, TF-IDF on sixteen, and MiniLM on fourteen. The most striking column is Incorrect: nine out of twenty closed-book answers, and zero out of forty with retrieval. For example, on Q16 closed-book said one hundred test sets, while both RAG answers correctly said twenty. Abstentions tell a second story. Closed-book was told to say so if it did not know, yet it never abstained. With retrieval, the model abstained five times. An abstention earns no credit, but it is better than a wrong value. Two cautions. RAG prompts also tell the model to acknowledge gaps, so prompt and context act together. And this review was AI-assisted. Both of us read and accepted it, but it was not a blind evaluation. We also ran each condition only once.
