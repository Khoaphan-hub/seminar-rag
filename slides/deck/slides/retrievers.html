[Phan Anh Khoa · 60 seconds] We compare two retrievers while keeping everything else the same. TF-IDF builds sparse vectors of word and phrase weights, so it rewards exact terms. MiniLM is a small pretrained model that turns each chunk into a dense vector with 384 dimensions. Notice that cosine is not specific to embeddings. Both methods normalize their vectors and use exact cosine similarity, so the difference comes from the representation. Both methods use the same chunks: 96 or 192 tokens with a 24-token overlap, which gives 81 or 36 chunks. Those sizes fit within MiniLM’s 256-token limit, so nothing is silently cut off. We evaluate k equal to one, three and five.
