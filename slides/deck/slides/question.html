CS420 Seminar S07 · RAG and Evidence Support · Phan Anh Khoa (24125059) · Bùi Trọng Thành (24125043)

[Phan Anh Khoa · 55 seconds] Hello everyone. If we give a language model the right document, can it still give a wrong or unsupported answer? That is the guiding question of our seminar on retrieval-augmented generation. To answer it, we separate three things that are often treated as one. Did the system find the evidence? Is the answer correct? And does the cited passage really support each claim? We test this on one open paper with twenty questions, comparing TF-IDF and MiniLM retrieval against a closed-book baseline. This is a small demonstration, so our claims stay within that scope. I will cover the background and the setup, and Thành will present the results.
